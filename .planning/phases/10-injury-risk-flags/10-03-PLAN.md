<objective>
Slice 2: add the D-05 joint-hyperextension flag — belle's stated Mode-3 priority ("내 자세가 이러면 부상 위험 높다"). This is the phase's single genuinely novel algorithm: distinguish normal flexion from reverse-bend (genu recurvatum / elbow hyperextension) by tracking the **cross-product sign in the segment-local sagittal plane**, because dot-product gives only direction-blind 0–180° magnitude. Knee and elbow are 1-DOF hinges, so this is valid.

The flag is absolute-signal (both modes), fires via the D-02 AND-gate with joint-localized control-loss (`unstable_body_parts`), and auto-renders through the `InjuryRiskSection` already built in 10-02. Absolute detection floors come from external clinical literature (knee >5° genu recurvatum; elbow >10° Beighton) tagged [CITED], with severity bands at 10°/20° — NOT curve-fit to the 13 videos (D-07 / Pitfall 3). The elite-no-FP invariant is re-asserted: 정은지's intentional extension must still read as flexion/straight, not hyperextension.

Purpose: deliver the Mode-3 core risk signal with calibrated, literature-sourced direction detection.
Output: D-05 firing rule in safety_flags.py; hyperextension tests GREEN; auto-rendered flag.
</objective>
