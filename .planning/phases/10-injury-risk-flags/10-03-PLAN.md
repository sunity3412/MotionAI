<objective>
Slice 2: add the D-05 joint-hyperextension flag — belle's stated Mode-3 priority ("내 자세가 이러면 부상 위험 높다"). This is the phase's single genuinely novel algorithm: distinguish normal flexion from reverse-bend (genu recurvatum / elbow hyperextension) by the SIGN of the hinge cross-product projected onto a deterministic body medial-lateral axis, because dot-product gives only direction-blind 0–180° magnitude. Knee and elbow are 1-DOF hinges whose flexion happens in the sagittal plane, so the cross-product (the hinge rotation axis) is parallel to the medial-lateral axis and its sign discriminates flexion vs reverse-bend. This is valid.

Codex (iter-2) flagged D-05's sign convention as still under-specified ("e.g. shoulder→hip" + a vague "known-flexion frame" left discretion in the most fragile part) and demanded real-keypoint coverage. This plan removes ALL discretion: ONE fixed body-frame construction, ONE deterministic flexion-calibration rule, an explicit fail-conservative branch for every ambiguity, and a MULTI-clip real-elite-keypoint regression (mirrored orientation, spin-around-pole, both limbs).

The flag is absolute-signal (both modes), fires via the D-02 LOCAL+TEMPORAL AND-gate with joint-localized control-loss (`unstable_body_parts` containing the specific knee/elbow key), and auto-renders through the `InjuryRiskSection` already built in 10-02. Absolute detection floors come from external clinical literature (knee >5° genu recurvatum; elbow >10° Beighton) tagged [CITED], severity bands at 10°/20° — NOT curve-fit to the 13 videos (D-07 / Pitfall 3).

Purpose: deliver the Mode-3 core risk signal with a deterministic, literature-sourced, conservatively-gated direction detector validated against MULTIPLE real elite clips.
Output: D-05 firing rule in safety_flags.py; hyperextension tests (synthetic + per-branch ambiguity + multi-clip real-elite incl. mirrored/spin/left-right) GREEN; auto-rendered flag.
